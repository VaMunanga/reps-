About REPS

Our story and philosophy

REPS encourages HIT students to turn intention into focused action through consistent practice and collaboration.

Planning is useful; repeated focused work produces progress. We track focused minutes and completed sessions.

Vision

A student community that stewards time intentionally, pursues excellence consistently and helps one another turn potential into action.

Mission

Provide simple systems, focused spaces and collaborative opportunities for studying, coding, building, discussion and reflection.

Objectives

  • Develop consistent work habits.
  • Encourage accountability and deliberate practice.
  • Connect productive peers across HIT programmes.

Weekly rhythm

Focus → Study → Discuss → Build → Reflect.

Plan intentional work, complete focused sessions, share progress and reflect on what to improve each week.

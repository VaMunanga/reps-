Why REPS

Progress needs a repeatable practice.

REPS is a proposed HIT student club built around focused work, peer learning, and practical follow-through.

The idea is simple.

Students often know what they want to improve. REPS gives that intention a rhythm and a group that values consistent effort.

Focus to reflection.

  1. FocusChoose one useful task and protect time for it.
  2. StudyBuild understanding before chasing output.
  3. DiscussTest ideas in a small, respectful group.
  4. BuildTurn the learning into something concrete.
  5. ReflectNotice what worked and plan the next rep.

What REPS can be

A student-led place for accountability, skill practice, thoughtful discussion, and project collaboration.

What it is not

A replacement for classes, a promise of grades, or a club that invents activity before it happens.
